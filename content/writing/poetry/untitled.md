<pre class="poem">

Dawn breaks in a way only known to sea ice
	under a seasons change;
		Under the knife known by its edge;
			And nothing less;		
				Slice; dear sun; and bring 
					forth the day; Rapturous decline;
						Sleep. 

		
But you will learn to love me;
	Fading into the light; All things revealed;	
		Hope of the night; Obsidian dreams concealed; Survive;
			Push through: Embrace; Make right; 
				She waits henceforth; As winter does; 
					As a predator; Gift giver; So hunt, or hunted. 

				
				
The night is her property;
	Tenancy leased and gifted; A mistress;
		Confined; Defined; Aligned; Inclined to
			worship; Breaking under the sun; Return.
				No end; Only pause as the wind does;
					As the ocean does;
						As we all do;

						
					And you will learn to love me.

</pre>
