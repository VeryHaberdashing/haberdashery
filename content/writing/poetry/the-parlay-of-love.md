"I met her there, upon my road to awe;

And watched the roses' shameful retreat;

For she became my world once more;

And parlayed me to lustful defeat;

Her eyes cut me as diamonds do;

Her voice was silence gold;

Yet in my worries all I could see

is us both as we grew old."
